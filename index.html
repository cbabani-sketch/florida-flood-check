Why the flood check matters

Flood insurance can add thousands of dollars a year to a Florida property, and a high-risk flood zone can change what a home is worth and how you can finance it. Since October 2025, Florida sellers must disclose known flood history, but the map is your first check. Look it up before you make an offer.
